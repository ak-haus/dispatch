## B12 — the home page load

Three causes, each measured; the row's "map-hero image cost" is refuted (Chrome has ignored
full-viewport images as LCP candidates since M88, so the map cannot be the element at all).

1. **The cover's text was server-rendered at `opacity: 0`** and revealed by Motion after the island
   hydrated, on delays of 0.1–2.4 s. LCP ignores paints at opacity 0 (M86), so what the metric
   reported was the reveal. web.dev budgets element render delay at under 10% of LCP; this was ~95%
   (TTFB 0.64 s against 11.9–13.2 s of render delay). A reader on a slow phone saw an empty map until
   the JS arrived. **Repair:** the cover renders visible. Only the wordmark keeps its load entry
   (CD5 §3.5), moved to CSS so it plays from first paint instead of waiting for JS; reduced motion now
   gets the static wordmark §3.5 asks for, which Motion's `reducedMotion="user"` did not give.
2. **The page was 506–514 px wide on a 412 px phone** — WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow — because of one nowrap
   caption in the build ticker (~510 px at 0.32em tracking). Chrome widens the initial viewport to fit
   overflow (measured: 514×1027), which pulled a below-the-fold heading into the LCP race. **Repair:**
   the caption wraps below `md`. Measured after: 320 px, 375 px and 412 px all fit, on all four
   surfaces.
3. **Six below-the-fold banners loaded eagerly** — 1.7 MB against the critical path, and Lighthouse
   charges every request that finished before the paint to LCP. **Repair:** `loading="lazy"`.

**Measured** (CI medians, faces served): home LCP **13628 → 6994–7908 ms**, bytes **2345 → 2009 KB**,
CLS 0.0000, and the LCP element moved from the volume label to the dek. Locally, against lhci's own
server, the observed LCP moved from 1.2 s (hydration + 0.4 s) to **190 ms — equal to first paint**.

### The instrument cannot see cause 1, so a test does

Served from localhost, every request finishes before the first paint, so Lantern's LCP graph holds all
of them: the simulated LCP read **13.59 s with the cover's text hidden and 13.66 s with it visible**,
and only the lazy images moved it (8.34 s). The ratchet is therefore a guard on eager bytes and the
critical path, not on render delay. `e2e/home.spec.ts` now measures the render delay itself — the LCP
entry must land within 250 ms of FCP and inside the cover — and the reflow at 320 px and 412 px. All
three fail on the previous code; the LCP guard fails with *no LCP entry at all*, because a Motion
reveal repaints nothing the metric records.

## Known properties, recorded

- **Lantern's lab LCP tracks what loaded before the paint.** Serve the page from localhost and that is
  every request, so this LCP behaves as an eager-byte budget in time units. It is why CI reads higher
  than production on early-painting surfaces (`/wire`: 2.9 s against 1.7 s) and why reader truth stays
  production Lighthouse.
- **Chrome's lazy-load distance depends on its connection estimate** — 1250 px on 4G, 3000 px when it
  cannot estimate (the localhost case), 2500 px on 3G. The same build therefore fetches a different
  number of banners in CI than on a phone.
- **Vercel preview deployments are SSO-protected**, so a pre-merge Lighthouse run against
  production-like hosting is not available without a bypass token; production is measured after the
  deploy.
