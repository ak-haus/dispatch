## The cadence defect — root cause, not just the rows

The brief's finding, confirmed: **closing merges never update their own flag rows.** Two live instances
this session, both in the same direction:

- **F10** reads *filed* on the board, but `AGENTS.md:30` already says "fifteen required contexts" — it
  was closed in passing by F17 at S8 and its own row never learned.
- **F3** read *open*, describing a condition B14 had already removed two days earlier.

And the two Build 15 inherited: F20's row dispatched a session after a lever disproved six hours
earlier; F16 said two branches when there were six.

The 2026-08-20 records sweep audits **doc** drift (file:line claims) and never touches the flag ledger,
so nothing catches this. But the sweep's absence is the symptom, not the cause. **The cause is
structural: the ledger lives outside the repo.** The Golden Board is an operator-held Artifact — that is
its established and correct design — which means no repo gate can read it, and "prose asks, gates
enforce" has nothing to bind to. Every ledger reconciliation to date has been an act of memory by
whichever session happened to notice.

The fix is therefore not another rule. It is an in-repo, machine-checkable flag ledger with the Artifact
as its presentation layer — filed below with a phase tag, because it is code-shaped and this is a
checkpoint.

**The interim rule, which costs nothing and closes the common case:** a PR that closes or changes a flag
names the flag ID in its title or body (this session's #27 does). A close ritual can then reconcile rows
against merged PRs by query instead of by recall.
