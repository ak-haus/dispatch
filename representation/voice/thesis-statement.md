## §1 — Thesis

### §1.1 — The substrate claim

DISpatch is Prime's V1 public-facing surface — the test pilot for Prime's multi-Building platform. The microsite ships as a beautifully-articulated dynamic editorial space; that is table stakes for principal-grade. Many such sites exist; many ship. The reason DISpatch ships is that it demonstrates, at miniature scale, the maximalist living-cartography that activates Prime's imagination across every future Building.

**The dispatch is a demo for the cartography. The cartography is the demo for Prime.**

The cartography is hero subtly, not loudly. It lives underneath the writing — alive, dynamic, mapped to where the reader is, what they are reading, what is near them, what is coming — and forgettable while writing or reading the article. Engagement is optional, presence is structural. Look up from the article and the cartography is there. This is the proof Prime's platform is offering: that a city of buildings, districts, neighborhoods, offices, citizens, and missives can hold together as one identity, expressed differently per Building, while sharing the substrate that makes the cohesion verifiable.

The thesis is calibrated to **principal-level expression** — Apple / Linear / Stripe / NYT / Quanta / Pentagram / Vercel / Anthropic at their best, expressed in DISpatch's register (architectural / editorial / neo-vintage / AI-forward — magazine-coded; marriage of NARRATIVE literary-civic register and META Wired/Hacker register via 5-typeface containment governance). The Apple-cohesion test (Tim-Cook-on-stage feel matches a CODA scene matches a "change your password" email matches the icon-corner-radius) gates this thesis-statement at the W1 close.

### §1.2 — What V1 ships

V1 ships the **building-and-below stack** of Prime's six-level cosmological-cartographic walking metaphor:

> Prime City → districts → buildings (DISpatch) → reception (DISpatch's homepage) → offices (META + NARRATIVE production units) → missives (articles)

- **City + districts** are forward-pointed in cartography (visible at the edge of any DISpatch surface; "anticipatory-not-missing" register per CD1 Concept 7); not surfaced as active UX (Prime City's homepage does not exist yet; other Buildings do not exist yet).
- **DISpatch (the building)** ships as the dev-diary magazine — the V1 working name encoding *DIS* (City of Dis from Inferno's lower hell; classical reference) + *dispatch* (magazine-thing; news-from-the-front) in one word.
- **Reception (homepage)** carries the magazine's glossary at the top — feature story, things to read today, ticker (live updates from Prime), coming-soon articles, Virgil chatbot, district map, building layout. The reader walks the path, sees what is happening in the offices, then clicks an article and sees the result of what is happening in those offices.
- **Offices (production units)** = META department + NARRATIVE department + Stream production units mapped to article patterns.
- **Missives (articles)** = output from the offices. Every article URL is treated as a self-contained, designed, citable magazine page (per master plan §3.B Astro Server Islands). The article IS the missive — designed as its own object, not a template skin.

### §1.3 — What V1 does not ship (and where those pieces live)

V1 does not ship Prime City's homepage, other Buildings, full Paradiso 9-charter-spheres content, or the remaining 6 Purgatorio Terraces (Roads / Districts / Neighborhoods / Offices / Citizens / Leisure formalization). Each of these is *forward-pointed* in cartography, scaffolded in folder structure (per CD3 Reframe 3 / Signal 011 — cosmology IS the literal top-level folder tree of Prime repo), and refines at its own per-Terrace-formalization arc. Editorial District is the V1 LOCKED first district within Districts-Terrace's content — the precedent for future-district pattern.

This deliberate scoping IS the thesis at work. The Burj Khalifa precedent: architecture and design WERE the marketing. DISpatch's building-and-below stack with city + districts forward-pointed is the architecture-as-marketing — the reader walks into a structure that visibly anticipates the larger city without trying to ship the larger city at V1.

### Concept 7 — Walking metaphor + parallel-native digital-print

**Universal concept.** The publication does not compromise digital for print or print for digital — it develops parallel-native formats where each is a complete expression of the same identity. The architecture of the publication IS the marketing of the publication (Burj Khalifa precedent: architecture and design WERE the marketing).

**Examples that prove the climb.** NYT Magazine 2026 (system works in print and digital without compromise; parallel-native formats). ZEIT Online (two-mode architecture: homepage as navigation; article as sanctuary). The Pudding (each article as its own designed object; per-article-as-self-contained-visual-system). The New Yorker (magazine glossary at homepage top — feature story + things to read + ticker + coming-soon — guides reader through editorial rhythm).

**Prime's expression.** A six-level cosmological-cartographic walking metaphor:

> Prime City → districts → buildings (DISpatch is one) → reception (DISpatch's homepage) → offices (META and NARRATIVE production units inside DISpatch) → missives (articles output FROM the building's offices)

The reader walks through the city, into the district, up to the building, through the reception, past the offices, and reads the missives. The article is not the building — the article is the **missive from the building**. The magazine, the dispatch itself, is the building. The offices are the production departments inside it. The citizens working in the offices author the missives.

**Two-mode architecture: porous gradient** (per CD1 Decision 8). Homepage-level affordances (ChapterRail, MetroMapMarker, metro-map "you are here") cross into the article surface to preserve civic-wayfinding from the city's map onto the building's interior. Strict article-as-sanctuary affordances (no SiteNav search input on article surface; reading-progress affordance; positionally-controlled cross-post buttons) preserve the reading register. CD4 spec.md per-component authoring controls the dilution by spec'ing exactly which affordances cross modes.
