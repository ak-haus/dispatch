## §1 — Universal pipeline concept

**A principal-grade editorial publication's pipeline holds three simultaneous disciplines without sacrificing any of them:** sustainable cadence (the writer wakes up Monday and writes; weekends are dark; years compound), quality at every artifact (every article passes the Apple-cohesion test; voice fidelity holds; DLDS provenance native), and structural amplification (the work reaches the audience graph automatically; canonical discipline holds; the crossfire mechanism multiplies discoverability).

Pipelines that optimize one of three at the expense of the others produce predictable failures: cadence-only pipelines burn the writer; quality-only pipelines never ship; amplification-only pipelines become content marketing without substance. The marriage is **AI-multiplier discipline** — Claude as structural co-author multiplies the writer's time-budget without compromising voice; multimedia as schema-level first-class field multiplies the article's reach without bolt-on overhead; DLDS provenance as native metadata multiplies regulatory readiness without retrofit cost.

**Examples that prove the concept at world-class scale:**

- **Stratechery (Ben Thompson)** — 12+ years of solo-author weekly free + 3 paid daily updates; sustainable through structural discipline (one register; one writer; one cadence; one publication; one tool stack); proves the cadence + quality marriage at multi-decade scale.
- **Bytes by ByteByteGo** — weekday-daily dev-content at solo-author scale; proves AI-multiplier + newsletter-first traction can compound to enterprise reach in <2 years.
- **The Pudding** — irregular cadence but per-article-as-designed-object; proves quality-first pipelines can earn audience without daily throughput.
- **NYT Magazine 2026** — semantic componentization at editorial-team scale; proves system-level componentization works at multipurpose-platform scope.
- **Stripe Press** — tentpole-piece register; long-form when ready; proves quality > frequency works for established-brand publications.

**Prime's expression — Stratechery-shaped hybrid + AI-multiplier + crossfire mechanism + DLDS native.** For DISpatch V1 traction posture (unknown brand; first many months free; goal = name in AI-developer space + Prime project promotion):

- **Cadence shape** — Stratechery-hybrid (5/7 days; thematic anchors per day; weekend dark). Per editorial-calendar.md §2.
- **AI-multiplier** — Claude Max flat-rate enables aggressive co-authorship at $0 marginal (`feedback_subscription_flat_cost_model.md`). META articles Claude-first; NARRATIVE articles Mayor-first with Claude expansion; transcript pieces Claude-extracts.
- **Crossfire** — replaces traditional social-share with multi-platform preview-card UX (Pentagram-positioning move; no shipped exemplar at principal grade per audience-engagement-model.md §3).
- **DLDS native** — every AI-touched asset carries IPTC 2025.1 + C2PA metadata; EU AI Act August 2 2026 ready at $0 incremental cost.

This runbook operationalizes that marriage into 7 phases, 12 per-story-type workflows, 4 quality gates, and 1 explicit AI-multiplier handoff protocol. The output is a pipeline a Mayor + Claude co-author can run inside a 1-2 hour daily window across 12+ years.

## §2 — The seven phases

The pipeline crosses seven phases between Monday-morning intention and post-publish telemetry. Each phase has a named entry condition, a named exit condition, a time-budget, and a named handoff actor. Phases compose linearly within a day but compose **non-linearly across a week** — multimedia composition for Monday NARRATIVE can begin Friday afternoon; LiveTicker chrome update runs continuously regardless of authoring phase.

### §2.1 — Phase 1: Pre-flight (5 min)

**Purpose.** Establish today's authoring context. The Mayor knows what they're writing before they open the editor; the Mayor knows what multimedia weight applies; the Mayor knows what lighthouse-arc position today's piece occupies.

| Field | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Entry condition** | Mayor opens daily content session (typically 9-11am window per `feedback_subscription_flat_cost_model.md` solo-dev workflow) |
| **Actor** | Mayor (solo; Claude not yet invoked) |
| **Time-budget** | 5 minutes |
| **Inputs** | Notion content calendar (today's date entry); editorial-calendar.md §2.1 weekly thematic-anchor cadence; first-six-months-slate.md lighthouse status; LiveTicker / cd-ledger surfacing (overnight commits + agent activity) |
| **Outputs** | Today's authoring context: story type + register + multimedia weight + cross-post tier-list + AI-multiplier handoff mode (Claude-first / Mayor-first / transcript-first / summary-first per §3) |
| **Exit condition** | Mayor has a one-line working title + story-type tag + register tag |
| **Handoff to** | Phase 2 (Source-gathering) |
| **Drift signal** | Pre-flight exceeds 10 minutes → Notion calendar is unclear OR story-type undecided → amendment review at week-close |

**Pre-flight checklist (Notion-readable; per §6 Notion content calendar template):**

- [ ] Today's date entry exists in Notion content calendar with story type tagged
- [ ] Story type matches editorial-calendar.md §2.1 day-of-week anchor (Mon NARRATIVE / Tue META / Wed NARRATIVE / Thu META / Fri retrospective) OR Mayor has explicit override reasoning
- [ ] Multimedia weight identified (light / medium / heavy) per story-types-catalog.md §3 per-type spec
- [ ] Cross-post tier list confirmed (Tier 1 mandatory; Tier 2 per content fit; HN/Reddit per strategic check)
- [ ] LiveTicker surfaced overnight signals (commits / agent dispatches / Mayor-direction events) — relevant to META days

### §2.2 — Phase 2: Source-gathering (Variable: 5-15 min)

**Purpose.** Assemble the raw material the author needs before composition begins. The asymmetry between META and NARRATIVE is structural here — META articles consume *artifacts* (commit logs, agent telemetry, validation cascade returns); NARRATIVE articles consume *experiences* (Mayor-Claude conversation transcripts, week-long thematic reflection, Mayor's institutional voice).

| Field | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Entry condition** | Phase 1 complete; story type known |
| **Actor** | Mayor + Claude (Claude active here; first AI-multiplier touch-point) |
| **Time-budget** | 5-15 minutes (META lower bound; NARRATIVE upper bound) |
| **Inputs** | Story-type-specific source per §3 AI-multiplier handoff table |
| **Outputs** | Source bundle staged in Claude context: artifact pointers (paths/SHAs/Linear-tickets) for META; transcript excerpts + thematic spine for NARRATIVE; per-story-type source per §4 |
| **Exit condition** | Claude has read the source bundle and Mayor has confirmed the article's THESIS in one sentence |
| **Handoff to** | Phase 3 (Authoring) |
| **Drift signal** | Source-gathering >20 min on a META day → source material missing OR story type mismatched → reconsider story type OR amend |

**Per-story-type source-gathering pattern (per §4 detail):**

| Story type bucket | Source material | Gather pattern |
|---|---|---|
| META: dispatch / change-log / infrastructure-decision / post-mortem / tooling adoption / performance investigation | git commits since last issue + Linear closed issues + Notion dispatch cards + validation cascade returns | Claude reads `git log --since="$LAST_PUBLISH"` + `gh issue list --state closed` + Notion dispatch-card pull |
| NARRATIVE: register-statement / philosophical / agent-creation / Mayor-direction / profile-piece / dev-diary | Mayor outline (5-7 min Mayor) + thematic-spine sentence + Mayor-Claude conversation transcripts from week (Claude extracts) | Mayor authors thesis sentence; Claude searches conversation logs for thematic resonance + extracts quotable moments |
| INTERPLAY: hybrid pieces (lighthouse arc) | Both META source + NARRATIVE outline; thesis pivot identified | Mayor + Claude jointly identify pivot — the META section anchors the mechanism; the NARRATIVE section anchors the institutional voice |
| FRIDAY RETROSPECTIVE | Week's commit log + LiveTicker aggregation + cd-ledger entries since Monday | Claude summarizes mechanically; Mayor adds Mayor-voice retrospective (5-10 min Mayor) |

### §2.3 — Phase 3: Authoring (Variable: 20-90 min per story type)

**Purpose.** Produce the article draft. This is the heaviest phase; the AI-multiplier handoff protocol (§3) governs WHO drafts and WHO edits. Per editorial-calendar.md §3.1 time-budget allocations:

| Story type | Time-budget | AI-multiplier mode |
|---|---|---|
| Monday NARRATIVE long-form | 75-105 min total (15-20 Mayor outline + 20-30 Claude expansion + 40-55 Mayor voice-overlay edit) | **Mayor-first** |
| Tuesday META dispatch | 20-35 min total (10-15 Claude draft from commits + 10-20 Mayor edit-and-verify) | **Claude-first** |
| Wednesday NARRATIVE process-piece | 50-75 min total (10-15 Claude transcript-extract + 35-50 Mayor voice-overlay) | **Transcript-first** |
| Thursday META data/research | 25-35 min total (10-15 Claude synthesis from cascade returns + 15-20 Mayor edit) | **Summary-first** |
| Friday retrospective | 15-25 min total (5-10 Claude summarize + 10-15 Mayor Mayor-voice add) | **Summary-first** |

**Field for the running draft:** `src/content/articles/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<slug>.mdx` (Astro Content Layer canonical location per master plan §3.B + §3.C). Article frontmatter populates `articleType` + `media[]` + `distribution` per master plan §3.C Zod schema as authoring progresses.

**Entry condition.** Phase 2 complete; thesis confirmed; source bundle staged.

**Exit condition.** Article body complete in MDX with frontmatter substantially populated (cover image slot can remain placeholder); story-type spec per story-types-catalog.md §2 satisfied; word-count within story-type target range.

**Handoff to.** Phase 4 (Multimedia composition) — note that lightweight inline multimedia (1 code block; 1 inline diagram) can author in-phase; heavyweight multimedia (cover image; data viz; scrollytelling sequence) defers to Phase 4.

**Drift signal.** Authoring >130% of story-type budget → AI-multiplier handoff protocol may be misapplied; voice-fidelity may have slipped; reconsider story type OR shorten scope. Mayor's call.

### §2.5 — Phase 5: Quality gates (5-10 min)

Cross-modal cohesion (visual + voice + multimedia + sonic) at gestalt level | Mayor (sole adjudicator; cannot be delegated per thesis-statement.md §5) | Apple-cohesion gestalt PASS; if NO, return to Phase 3 OR Phase 4 with specific gestalt gap named | `thesis-statement.md` §5 Apple-cohesion test |
| **G4 — DLDS provenance band** | DldsPanel renders correctly; C2PA credentials visible where Firefly fired; ai_role attribution accurate per actual co-authorship split | Pre-publish preview validation | Provenance band visible + accurate + per `feedback_brand_track_dlds_locked.md` three-layer | `feedback_brand_track_dlds_locked.md` + EU AI Act August 2 2026 |

**Quality gate ordering:** G1 fires automatically pre-commit (zero Mayor cost). G2 fires next (Mayor's per-artifact Reviewer pass; ~3-5 min). G3 fires at preview render (Mayor reads-as-reader; ~2-3 min). G4 fires last as automated render-check (~1 min).

## §3 — AI-multiplier handoff protocol (load-bearing)

This is the operational core of the pipeline. The 1-2 hour daily window only holds if the Mayor-Claude handoff is structurally disciplined — the wrong handoff mode on a given story type doubles the time-budget. This section names the modes; §4 names which story types use which.

### §3.1 — Four handoff modes

The pipeline supports four AI-multiplier modes. Each has a named entry, a named division of labor, and a named exit condition. Mayor cannot mix modes within a single article without re-authoring; mode selection is Phase 1 work.

| Mode | Who initiates | Mayor's heaviest phase | Claude's role | Typical story types |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mayor-first** | Mayor writes outline (thesis sentence + section headers + per-section thematic spine) | Voice-overlay editing pass over Claude expansion | Expands Mayor outline into full draft preserving outline structure + section ordering; surfaces ambiguities for Mayor resolution; does NOT introduce thesis | Monday NARRATIVE long-form; lighthouse-arc pieces; Mayor-direction posts; register-statement posts |
| **Claude-first** | Claude drafts from source material (commit logs / agent telemetry / dispatch returns) | Edit-and-verify (factual accuracy + voice + tightening) | Generates structural draft from artifacts; cites source per Vale.sh DLDS lane-schema; flags uncertainty inline | Tuesday META dispatch; Thursday META data/research; change-log; tooling adoption; performance investigation |
| **Transcript-first** | Claude searches Mayor-Claude conversation logs and extracts narrative arc | Voice-overlay (heavy edit; arc-shaping; institutional anchor work) | Surfaces quotable moments + thematic resonance from week's transcripts; Mayor reshapes into NARRATIVE arc | Wednesday NARRATIVE process-piece; dev-diary entries; agent-creation framing |
| **Summary-first** | Claude summarizes mechanical week-aggregation (commits + cd-ledger + LiveTicker) | Mayor-voice retrospective add (Mayor adds reflective commentary atop Claude's mechanical summary) | Aggregates and condenses; produces neutral mechanical baseline; does NOT editorialize | Friday retrospective; quarterly Mayor-direction (Mayor-voice atop Claude-aggregation); annual cosmology reveal |

### §3.2 — Handoff contract per mode

Each mode has explicit input/output contracts the Mayor and Claude both honor. Violation of contract = mode mismatch = time-budget breach.

#### §3.2.1 — Mayor-first contract

```yaml
mayor_first:
  mayor_inputs:
    - thesis_sentence: required, ≤25 words, single declarative
    - section_outline: required, 4-8 section headers, each ≤8 words
    - thematic_spine_per_section: required, 1-2 sentences per section indicating the through-line
    - register_lock: required, NARRATIVE_primary | NARRATIVE_with_META_pivot | hybrid_interplay_pattern
  claude_outputs:
    - full_draft: in MDX, preserving section_outline verbatim (Mayor approves any new sub-section)
    - inline_voice_anchors_cited: cite library/voice/anchors.md when register-bearing phrases used
    - ambiguity_flags: inline {{?}} markers where Claude couldn't resolve from Mayor inputs
    - thesis_preserved: thesis_sentence appears in opening + closing pivots; never re-articulated as Claude's framing
  mayor_voice_overlay:
    - pass_1: read-through; collapse Claude verbosity; substitute Mayor diction
    - pass_2: address {{?}} ambiguity flags
    - pass_3: register-coherence check (does this read as Mayor, not Claude?)
  contract_breach_signals:
    - Claude introduces new thesis without Mayor approval
    - Claude restructures section_outline silently
    - Mayor cannot recognize own voice in final article after voice-overlay
```

#### §3.2.2 — Claude-first contract

```yaml
claude_first:
  source_inputs:
    - commit_log_since_last_publish: git log --since="$LAST_PUBLISH" --format="..."
    - linear_closed_issues: gh issue list --state closed --search "..."
    - validation_cascade_returns: relevant cd-ledger/dispatches/*/returns/*.md
    - dispatch_cards_relevant: Notion query for status=Done since last publish
  claude_outputs:
    - structural_draft: in MDX with META-pattern frontmatter (compressed metadata band per article-patterns/meta-pattern.md)
    - artifact_citations: every claim cites SHA OR Linear-ticket-id OR cascade-return path
    - uncertainty_markers: inline {{verify: <what>}} where Claude is < 90% confident
    - meta_register_lock: compressed-prose register per article-patterns/meta-pattern.md; no NARRATIVE-style anchor language
  mayor_edit_and_verify:
    - artifact_citation_check: every {{verify}} resolved by Mayor; SHA/ticket/path matches reality
    - register_check: compressed META register held throughout; no NARRATIVE drift
    - tighten_pass: Mayor edits for compression (META target word count enforced)
  contract_breach_signals:
    - Claude introduces speculative claims without artifact citation
    - Claude drifts into NARRATIVE register (anchor language, institutional voice)
    - {{verify}} markers exceed 5 per article → source bundle was insufficient
```

#### §3.2.3 — Transcript-first contract

```yaml
transcript_first:
  source_inputs:
    - mayor_claude_conversations_since_last_narrative: searchable conversation log for the week
    - thematic_seed: one-sentence theme Mayor identifies in Phase 1 (e.g., "the week we ratified CD7")
  claude_outputs:
    - thematic_extraction_doc: bulleted list of quotable moments + thematic resonance points + emotional arc markers from transcripts
    - draft_arc_proposal: 3-5 narrative-arc shapes Mayor can choose from (each ~80-120 words)
    - quote_inventory: verbatim quotes Claude flags as potentially article-bearing (Mayor selects subset)
  mayor_arc_selection_and_voice_overlay:
    - arc_select: Mayor picks one of 3-5 proposed arcs OR overrides with custom arc
    - voice_overlay: Mayor authors prose around the selected arc; Claude provides structural support
    - quote_curation: Mayor selects quotes; Claude provides context-stitch sentences
  contract_breach_signals:
    - Claude editorializes quotes (adds framing Mayor didn't author)
    - Arc proposals don't preserve emotional arc from transcripts
    - Mayor finds themself rewriting Claude's structural support beyond 30% threshold (signal: arc selection was wrong)
```

#### §3.2.4 — Summary-first contract

```yaml
summary_first:
  source_inputs:
    - week_commit_log: git log --since="last Friday" --format="..."
    - liveticker_aggregation: cd-ledger entries since last publish
    - dispatch_cards_done_this_week: Notion query
  claude_outputs:
    - mechanical_summary: pure aggregation; no editorial framing; what-shipped + what-surfaced + what's-coming
    - liveticker_subset: 5-10 highlighted activity entries Mayor reviews
  mayor_mayor_voice_overlay:
    - retrospective_add: Mayor adds Mayor-voice retrospective commentary (the "Mayor's perspective" sections; Friday retrospective register)
    - this_week_next_week: Mayor previews next week thematic anchor
  contract_breach_signals:
    - Claude editorializes mechanical summary (adds qualifiers / opinions / projections)
    - LiveTicker subset doesn't surface most engagement-relevant entries
    - Mayor-voice overlay reads as Claude-with-Mayor-stamp rather than Mayor-with-Claude-support
```

### §3.3 — Mode transitions within a day

A single day's article should hold one mode start-to-finish. **Mid-article mode-switching doubles the time-budget** because the contract changes. The exception: lighthouse-arc INTERPLAY pieces (e.g., "Crossfire mechanism reveal") that explicitly hold META + NARRATIVE pivot — these run **Mayor-first** with the META pivot section running a sub-Claude-first contract within Mayor's outline (Claude expands the META mechanism section from source artifacts; Mayor authors the NARRATIVE framing sections directly).

### §3.4 — Mode-selection decision rule

When story type is ambiguous (rare; story-types-catalog.md covers most cases), Mayor selects mode by answering one question: **"What's the heaviest authoring labor?"**

- If the heaviest labor is voice / institutional framing / register-bearing prose → Mayor-first.
- If the heaviest labor is artifact synthesis / mechanism explanation / technical compression → Claude-first.
- If the heaviest labor is arc-shaping from existing dialogue → Transcript-first.
- If the heaviest labor is mechanical aggregation Mayor reflects atop → Summary-first.

## §5 — Quality gates (4 gates expanded)

Quality gates are GATES not TARGETS per `feedback_metric_vs_gate_discipline.md`. Each gate has a binary pass/fail with named failure routing. No gate is "scored"; no aggregate quality number gets computed; per-deliverable justification is the discipline.

### §5.1 — G1: Structural (Vale.sh) — pre-commit, automated

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **What it validates** | DLDS YAML frontmatter complete (lane-schema per `library/voice/lane-schema.md` §5); canonical_url present and structurally valid URL; ai_role attribution present per per-article actual co-authorship split; Vale.sh voice-fidelity rules (no banned phrases per voice anchors; lane-schema disclosure complete) |
| **When it fires** | Pre-commit hook on every article file write to `src/content/articles/` |
| **Pass criterion** | All Vale.sh rules return zero violations; DLDS YAML schema validates; canonical_url URL-parseable |
| **Failure routing** | Pre-commit fails; Mayor returns to Phase 3 (Authoring) to address; structural errors are mechanically resolvable |
| **Reference** | `.vale.ini` config; `library/voice/lane-schema.md`; `feedback_brand_track_dlds_locked.md` three-layer |

### §5.2 — G2: Per-artifact Reviewer — Mayor (Era 1) or agent (Era 2)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **What it validates** | Voice fidelity (does it sound like Prime?); register coherence (META vs NARRATIVE held throughout; no drift); story-type spec compliance (per story-types-catalog.md §3 template); thesis integrity (single thesis maintained; no thesis-drift mid-article) |
| **When it fires** | Phase 5 explicit pass; Mayor reads-through-as-Reviewer (different lens than reads-through-as-author) |
| **Pass criterion** | Mayor signs off in Notion content-calendar entry checkbox; named-issue list empty |
| **Failure routing** | Mayor returns to Phase 3 with specific named issues (voice slip / register drift / thesis fracture); does NOT proceed to G3 without G2 pass |

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|

## §8 — The time-budget covenant

The pipeline only holds if the daily window holds. Per `feedback_subscription_flat_cost_model.md` + editorial-calendar.md §3.1 — 1-2 hour daily window is **non-negotiable**. Years compound only if days are sustainable.

### §8.1 — Per-day time-budget summary

| Day | Story type | Pipeline-phase total | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | NARRATIVE long-form (#8 / #10 / #11 / #12) | 100-150 min (1.7-2.5hr) | Heaviest day; Mayor-first mode; Mayor voice-overlay is the bulk; lighthouse-arc pieces fire Monday |
| **Tue** | META dispatch (#1) / infrastructure-decision (#2) | 35-60 min | Claude-first; short Mayor edit; quick crossfire |
| **Wed** | NARRATIVE process-piece (#7) / agent-creation (#9) | 80-115 min | Transcript-first; medium Mayor voice-overlay |
| **Thu** | META data/research (#5) / (#6) | 40-70 min | Claude-first or Summary-first; data viz adds time but caps at 60-75 |
| **Fri** | Retrospective (#1 variant) | 25-50 min | Summary-first; lightest day; week-close + drift review |
| **Sat** | Dark | 0 min | Sustainability-protected |
| **Sun** | Dark | 0 min | Sustainability-protected |
| **Weekly total** | — | 280-445 min (4.7-7.4 hr/week) | Sub-1hr/day average across 5/7 days |

**The covenant.** Mayor commits to weekend dark; pipeline commits to delivering quality at the time-budget; AI-multiplier discipline holds. Violation of any one breaks the covenant.

### §8.2 — Drift indicators per editorial-calendar.md §3.3

If any of these surface, pipeline shape is failing and routes to amendment review:

- Daily time-budget regularly exceeds 2.5 hours → AI-multiplier handoff misapplied OR scope-creep per article OR mode-mismatched
- Weekend dark broken 3+ weekends in a quarter → cadence not sustainable; pull back to 4/7 day cadence (Tue META + Wed NARRATIVE + Thu META + Fri retrospective) and re-evaluate
- Quality gate failures on >20% of week's output → voice-fidelity slipping OR Apple-cohesion drift OR story-type mismatch
- Crossfire variant generation >20 min/day → Stream 4 automation friction; refer to automation-requirements.md B3
- Mayor reads Claude output and cannot recognize own voice in final article (post voice-overlay) → mode mismatch OR Claude-context-drift; check handoff protocol contract per §3.2
