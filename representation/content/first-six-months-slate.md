## §1 — Universal lighthouse-piece concept

**Lighthouse pieces are the early-arc stories that establish what the publication is about.** They demonstrate the discipline at the lattice-template level; they prove the mechanism the publication is selling; they articulate institutional direction; they reveal the cosmology the brand inhabits. Lighthouse pieces fire first because they're how unknown brands earn attention — by showing immediately what the publication is for.

**Examples that prove the concept at world-class scale:**

- **Stratechery's launch series (2013)** — Aggregation Theory + Stack Discipline articulated Ben Thompson's framework that became the publication's operating thesis
- **Stripe Press's "Working in Public"** — established what Stripe Press was about by shipping the manifesto early
- **The Pudding's "What's Going On In This Graph?" launch** — proved the per-article-as-designed-object claim by being one
- **The Atlantic's 1857 inaugural** — declared "of no party or clique" institutional anchor; signal-to-the-reader-of-what-the-publication-is

**Prime's expression — 5-piece lighthouse arc + ~25-30 weekly-cadence stories drawn from candidate pool.** Lighthouse pieces ship in V1 launch arc (Q3 2026); weekly-cadence stories fill the 6-month runway per Stratechery-hybrid cadence (Mon NARRATIVE long-form + Tue META dispatch + Wed NARRATIVE process + Thu META data + Fri retrospective; Sat/Sun dark) per `editorial-calendar.md` §2.
