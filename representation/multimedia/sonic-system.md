# DISpatch — Sonic System v0

## §0 — Reading discipline

Per master plan §0 C7+C8+C9 + thesis-statement §0:

- **Concept-first.** Universal sonic-design concepts cited first (sonic-logo as audible wordmark; audio-palette as ambient sonic register; voice-talent guidelines as register direction); reference platforms cited as examples that prove the concept holds at world-class scale; non-binding for future Buildings.
- **Muted technical language preserved** for specific instrumentation choices / specific composers / specific DAWs / specific voice talent — Mayor adjudicates at W4 Mayor-gated cascade per master plan §6.5 5-dimension framework.
- **Reduced-motion-respect FLOOR is NON-NEGOTIABLE** — sonic content respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-reduced-data` user preferences per WCAG 2.1 + WCAG 2.2 + W3C Sound Design accessibility guidance; user-initiated playback ONLY for ambient sonic content; no autoplay of substantive audio.
- **Per-Building register flexibility preserved** — DISpatch's V1 sonic-system is one Building's choice for one surface's purpose. Future Prime Buildings choose per their purpose per master plan §1.3 5-narrow-certification gates.

## §1 — Sonic-system universal concept

A sonic-system is the **audible equivalent of the visual identity** — the publication's voice as audio. It carries the same brand DNA (register, atmosphere, institutional weight, walking metaphor) into the audio register so cross-modal cohesion holds: when a reader reads a DISpatch article and listens to a DISpatch podcast and watches a DISpatch screencast and uses Virgil chatbot voice mode, the experience reads as one publication, expressed differently per medium.

The sonic-system parallels the typographic 5-typeface containment governance: each sonic component owns a register-function (audible identity / ambient register / spoken-word register); the combination creates a multi-voice sonic publication where the sonic register IS the wayfinding signal before the listener parses words.

**Examples that prove the concept at world-class scale:**

- **Apple's sonic identity** (TV+ / Music / podcasts) — sonic-logo at brief flash + atmospheric ambient at long-form + voice-talent direction at editorial podcasts. Cross-modal cohesion across hardware unboxing audio, TV+ original-series intro, Music Beats 1 station idents, podcast app interface sounds.
- **NPR / This American Life sonic identity** — voice-talent direction (Ira Glass et al. recognizable register) + ambient soundbed register + station identity logo. The listener registers the publication before the words land.
- **NYT The Daily / The Run-Up sonic identity** — institutional voice-talent register + ambient newsroom soundbed + sonic-logo intro/outro. NARRATIVE-register editorial podcasts at frontier scale.
- **Pentagram's sonic-branding work** (Mastercard / Airbnb / Sotheby's) — sonic-logo as load-bearing brand identity; the visual + sonic systems composed at construction.
- **Stripe's product audio** (interface sounds; checkout success tones) — restrained sonic identity at product-surface scale; the sound IS the product's institutional signal.
- **Anthropic's Claude voice mode** — voice-talent register direction for AI assistant; the voice IS the model's editorial register.

These platforms prove the sonic-system universal concept holds at frontier scale; they DO NOT prescribe Prime's per-surface sonic register. Future Prime Buildings choose per their purpose.

## §3 — Audio-palette (ambient sonic register)

### §3.1 — Concept + universal expression

**The audio-palette is the sonic equivalent of the typographic + chromatic palette.** It defines the ambient sonic register — music character, ambient texture vocabulary, mood/register mapping per surface family. Where the visual atmospheric chrome (`--sky-high` / `--sky-low` / `--reflect` / `--window-warm`) defines warmth at the visual layer, the audio-palette defines warmth at the sonic layer.

Audio-palette content lands at:

- **NARRATIVE article ambient `audioBlock`** — per `narrative-pattern.md` §3.5 atmospheric audio integration (sonic-system audio-palette NARRATIVE register; W4+ activation).
- **META article ambient `audioBlock`** — rare per `meta-pattern.md` §3.5; only at interplay sections per `interplay-pattern.md` Pattern A (META-primary with NARRATIVE pivot).
- **LiveRoom ambient soundbed** — per CD4 #20 LiveRoom (production opt-in); ambient register holds across multi-author session presence.
- **CartographyCanvas ambient soundbed** — forward-arc; cartography substrate may carry subtle ambient sonic equivalent of the visual flicker (per CD3 §4.6 atmospheric texture; per CD5 §4 atmospheric layer motion-coupling rule). Currently inclined toward NOT shipping cartography ambient at V1 (atmosphere-not-spectacle discipline; reduced-data + reduced-motion floor); future arc may revisit.

## §4 — Voice-talent guidelines (spoken-word register direction)

### §4.1 — Concept + universal expression

**Voice-talent guidelines define the register for spoken-word Prime content** — VirgilChat voice mode, screencast voiceover, podcast narration if Prime publishes podcasts, accessibility-driven audio description tracks. Where the typographic Title + Body slots define DISpatch's editorial reading register, the voice-talent guidelines define DISpatch's editorial spoken register. The voice IS the publication's editorial register translated to spoken word.

The voice-talent register matches the **Magazine-not-newspaper register** per CD1 Decision 1 (Mayor 2026-05-08) + thesis-statement §1.1: marriage of NARRATIVE literary-civic + META Wired/Hacker via 5-typeface containment governance translated to audio. Voice-talent direction carries the literary register primary; technical-precision register appears at META-content surfaces (terminal-search audio results / code-walkthrough screencasts).
