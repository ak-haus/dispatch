### §2.1 — Hue dial governance rule (RATIFIED — Hybrid C+D)

**The rule.** Hues live in **scope-locked tiers** (containment governance, structural twin to the 5-typeface containment governance from CD1 Concept 1); each hue addition within a tier requires **concept-citation evidence** (citation gate, structural twin to C7 concept-first reference discipline). The combination is the load-bearing meta-rule for the system: every downstream call (lightness scale, chroma envelope, lane pigments, chrome tokens, wordmark, future Building hue requests) inherits from this one rule.

**Why hybrid.** Tier C (containment) carries the structural cohesion mechanism — the Apple-cohesion test passes by partition-not-blend, identical to typography. Citation D (concept-cited additions within a tier) prevents "vibes pigments" — every hue that lands must express a universal concept that examples-prove at world-class scale, mirroring the discipline already operating at thesis tier.

**The four tiers.**

| Tier | Scope | Containment lock | Addition gate (citation strictness) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prime-platform-wide** | Hues that appear across Buildings (e.g., institutional anchor pigment if wordmark resolves dispatch-red; nav-state hues; Prime brand-DNA carriers) | Token names appear in `tokens.color.platform.*` namespace; cross-Building visible | **Strictest** — universal-concept citation + reference-archive prior art + cascade-source evidence + Mayor adjudication. Same bar as the 5-typeface Nav slot. |
| **DISpatch-locked** | Hues bound to this Building's register marriage (lane pigments terracotta / graphite-blue / dispatch-red live here; Body/Title surface hues) | Token names appear in `tokens.color.dispatch.*` namespace; not visible to other Buildings' token compilations | **Strict** — cited to thesis universal concept + reference-archive prior art; brand `stewardship/` Reviewer certifies before token lands. |
| **Asset-locked** | Hues bound to assets that carry their own typography (cartography labels, civic markers, signage — paired with Civic/Dante typeface slot per CD1 Concept 1) | Token names appear in `tokens.color.asset.*` namespace; consumed only by asset-rendering surfaces | **Strict in citation** but **flexible in palette** — cartography wine-red infra-pulse marker (asset-tier inheritor of Prime accent showing Inferno-infra surfacing through Purgatorio-terraces); future cartography atmospheric inks; cited to cartography concept + asset-typography concept. |
| **Component-locked** | Hues bound to specific components and never escape (DLDS panel chrome, terminal-search highlight, code-block syntax tokens, Meta/code typeface backgrounds) | Token names appear in `tokens.color.component.<component-name>.*` namespace; cannot be referenced outside the named component | **Lighter** — cited to component spec.md rationale; per-component Reviewer certifies via component addition workflow. Scope-lock already constrains drift; citation rigor calibrates accordingly. |

**Tier inheritance.** Hues do NOT inherit across tiers — a Prime-platform-wide hue is its own token; a DISpatch-locked hue is its own token; the rendered values may coincidentally match across tiers if Mayor wishes (e.g., dispatch-red wordmark + dispatch-red lane pigment per Section 5 Option B), but they are encoded as distinct tokens with distinct provenance and distinct addition gates. ### §2.3 — Chroma envelope governance (RATIFIED — per-hue parabolic envelopes per Tailwind v4)

**The rule.** Adopt **Tailwind v4's per-hue parabolic chroma envelopes verbatim** for sRGB + P3 gamut safety. Each hue family carries its own parabolic envelope — peak chroma at L≈0.5–0.7 (the perceptual "vivid" mid-range per the hue's natural gamut peak), tapering toward 0 at L→0 and L→1. Yellow's chroma peak skews high-L (≈0.85); red's skews mid-L (≈0.55); blue's skews low-mid-L (≈0.45) — Tailwind v4's published OKLCH values calibrate this per hue.

**Why per-hue not flat-shared.** Real-world OKLCH gamuts cannot hit max chroma at every lightness — sRGB / P3 monitors physically can't display "vivid red at L=0.95" or "vivid yellow at L=0.05." A flat shared cap (γ Adobe Spectrum-style) forces the lowest-common-denominator envelope; per-hue envelopes let each hue reach its natural gamut peak. This matters specifically for the §4 lane pigments — terracotta, graphite-blue, and dispatch-red each have different gamut peaks, and a flat cap would force one of them to render below its natural vibrancy.

**Per-tier chroma discipline.**

- **Prime-platform-wide tier** — full Tailwind v4 per-hue envelopes; broad expressive range for cross-Building consumption.
- **DISpatch-locked tier (lane pigments)** — Tailwind v4 per-hue envelopes for terracotta / graphite-blue / dispatch-red hue families; per-hue values land at §4 lane pigment finalization.
- **DISpatch-locked tier (Vellum atmospheric scale)** — explicit low-chroma envelope (chroma ≤ 0.06 per step; warm-paper substrate register requires restraint); chrome tokens are NOT vivid surfaces.
- **Asset-locked tier (cartography wine-red infra-pulse markers)** — wine-red is asset-tier inheritor of Prime accent (Inferno-infra surfacing through Purgatorio-terraces); high-chroma envelope per cartography register (D&D-map + GTA-5-zoom + fictional-modern-city facsimile per CD1 thesis §3); per-asset chroma calibrated at CD3 cartography ratification (CD2 publishes stub `var(--asset-cartography-pulse, oklch(0.45 0.18 22°))` per §9 stub interface).
- **Component-locked tier (DLDS panels / terminal-search highlights / Meta-code surfaces)** — variable chroma per component spec.md per §1.1 muted-language; calibrated at CD4 component-visual ratification.

**Validation tooling.** chroma.js for programmatic envelope computation + per-hue gamut linting; Leonardo Color for APCA Lc validation per pairing; Realtime Colors for live-preview cross-validation; Mayor manual cascade as Layer 6 per `feedback_external_validation_distinct_layer.md`.

## §3 — APCA contrast validation against thesis surfaces (RATIFIED — closes DP-Q3)

Per master-plan §11 **DP-Q3** (APCA contrast verification — never run before; **CD2 closes DP-Q3 here**). Decision 4 (§10) bundles five sub-ratifications below.

### §3.1 — APCA commitment

**The rule.** APCA Lc is the canonical contrast metric for all DISpatch surfaces. WCAG 2.1 ratio retained ONLY as fallback for tools that can't compute APCA (axe-core's default; CI baseline per master-plan §6.6 Row 6 axe-core compliance). Per pairing, both metrics computed; **APCA Lc is the gate**.

**Why APCA over WCAG 2.1.** WCAG 2.1's `(L1+0.05)/(L2+0.05)` ratio is luminance-only and polarity-blind — over-passes light-on-light (yellow text on white may pass numerically but be illegible), over-fails dark-on-dark, doesn't model spatial frequency (small text vs large display). APCA models perceptual contrast (lightness-difference + spatial-frequency + polarity-aware) per the Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm (SAPC) lookup tables published 2022-2024; outputs Lc score (-108 to +106; sign indicates polarity). APCA is the 2026 frontier per `feedback_concept_first_examples_prove_muted_language.md`:

- Quanta Magazine — APCA-validated editorial palette
- NYT Magazine 2026 — APCA-disciplined
- Emerging WCAG 3.0 standard inherits APCA's perceptual model

**Floor vs principal-grade.** Standard APCA minimums (Lc ≥75 body / ≥60 display / ≥45 incidental) are the *floor* — passes the spec. Principal-grade publications empirically run +10 Lc above floor (Quanta body Lc 80-85; NYT Mag 2026 body Lc ~80-85; Sotheby's institutional copy Lc 85+). DISpatch targets principal-grade per master-plan §1.7 reframe 2.1.
