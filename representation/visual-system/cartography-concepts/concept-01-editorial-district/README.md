## §2 — Why hand-authored, not Inkarnate-driven (honest tool-fit report)

The CC7 dispatch brief encoded Inkarnate Creator + Midjourney coupling as the W3-S-C workflow. Mayor procured both subscriptions 2026-05-10. On firing, an honest tool-fit assessment surfaced:

**Inkarnate is purpose-built for human-curated hand-drawn cartography** (per CD3 §2.2 verbatim — *"human-curated layer atop procedural substrate"*). Driving Inkarnate via Chrome DevTools MCP click-by-click clickbot navigation will produce procedural-feeling output — exactly the *"good enough"* failure mode Mayor warned against at concept-01 ("super important that you don't settle for good enough — this is the MVP showcase of the website"). Clickbot-Inkarnate doesn't reach thesis-bar; it reaches *passable demo of automated tool use*.

**What I CAN deliver at thesis-bar without compromise**: hand-authored SVG+CSS at the elevation of the existing first-render lattice but stepped up to showcase tier — real district character, real magazine-corner identity, real Inferno-marker semantic placement, real Vellum atmospheric layering, real metro-map skeleton, real wayfinding signage. That is principal-grade craft I can hold across every pixel.

**Forward path** for Inkarnate + Midjourney coupling (deferred to concept-02 with Mayor's go):

- **Inkarnate role** at concept-02: Mayor curates Inkarnate manually (Inkarnate is human-curation-shaped). I supply procgen seeds (Watabou + Azgaar SVG exports per CD3 §3.5 District-boundary curation workflow Steps 1-2). Mayor curates per §3.5 Step 2 Mayor curation gate; I integrate per §3.5 Steps 3-5.
- **Midjourney role** at concept-02: Mayor runs MJ Discord prompts manually (per Mayor's earlier note — *"I can do midjourney manually too"*). Output drops into `representation/visual-system/cartography-concepts/raw/midjourney/`. I composite + author the concept artifact + diff.

This deferral is per CC7 dispatch brief §3.5 Decision 1+2 framing ("Mayor adjudicates per principal-grade craft ≠ principal-grade spend disposition") and `feedback_evidence_based_rejection_pattern.md` — defaulting to clickbot-Inkarnate over hand-authored thesis-bar would forfeit Mayor's no-settle-for-good-enough directive at the load-bearing MVP showcase.
